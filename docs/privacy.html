Ludo Elemental — Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Ludo Elemental is a board game. This page explains what data the app handles, why, and how to delete it. We do not show ads, we do not sell data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Playing offline

Pass N Play, with friends or computer players, needs no account. Your settings, the cosmetics you have equipped and whether you have seen the tutorial are saved only on your device.

Accounts

Online play needs an account. You can choose:

  • Google sign-in — we receive your name, email address and profile picture link from Google.
  • Guest — an anonymous account with a random ID. No email, no name from anywhere else.

For either kind we store a player name, a profile picture (for guests, a photo you choose from your gallery, resized small) and your account ID. Accounts are hosted by Supabase.

Online games

When you play online, the game state, room code and the players in the room are stored and sent between devices through Supabase. Other players in your game see your player name and profile picture.

Purchases

The shop sells cosmetic game boards as one-time purchases. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. Purchase records are processed by RevenueCat, which checks the receipt and tells the app what you own. RevenueCat receives an app user ID (your account ID, or a random ID if you are not signed in), your purchase history and basic device information such as OS version and country.

Feedback

If you send a message with "Contact Developer", the message and your player name are delivered to us by Formspree. Do not include anything you would not want us to read.

Permissions

The app uses the internet for online play and purchases. It opens your photo gallery only when a guest chooses to change their picture, and only reads the one photo you pick. It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Children

Ludo Elemental is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings, tap Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, player name, profile picture, matchmaking entry and the online rooms you host, straight away.

Without the app: email us at the developer address shown on the Ludo Elemental Google Play listing, from the email of your Google account or with your player name, and ask for deletion. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as required for payments, refunds and tax; you can still restore purchased boards from the shop with the same Google Play account. Data saved only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We keep account data, and the online rooms you host, until you delete your account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: use "Contact Developer" in the app's Settings, or the developer email on the Google Play listing.
